Our Story

Built on 26 Years of Executive Experience

Our CEO and President, Joe Mulieri, spent 26 years as an executive at a major U.S. corporation. After completing his career in the private sector, Joe became interested in air quality issues, specifically mold and its effects on health.

That interest led Joe to get certified as a Mold Inspector and Remediator. The quality of his work and the thoroughness of his process quickly set MoldGone apart. Because of Joe's business ethics, MoldGone grew in reputation and expanded its service areas throughout Maryland, Virginia, and the District of Columbia.

Today, MoldGone employs 11 full-time staff members. All inspectors and management hold degrees from four-year institutions, and our certifications and credentials are among the highest and most respected in the industry.
