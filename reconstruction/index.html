Why Hire a Separate Contractor When We Do Both?

Many remediation companies stop after the mold or water damage is addressed, leaving you with stripped walls and exposed framing. You're then stuck finding a general contractor, coordinating schedules, and hoping they understand what was done. That's not how we work.

MoldGone technicians are required to have construction experience because so many of our projects involve both demolition and reconstruction. You can use MoldGone as a single point of service for the entire project, saving you the inconvenience and cost of hiring a separate construction company.

What We Rebuild

Full-Scope Reconstruction Services

Every element removed during remediation is restored to match or exceed the original quality. We handle everything so your space looks and feels like new.

Drywall & Framing

New drywall installation, taping, mudding, and texturing to seamlessly match your existing walls and ceilings. Structural framing repairs when needed.

Flooring

Hardwood, tile, luxury vinyl, carpet, and laminate installation. We source matching materials and ensure proper subfloor preparation for lasting results.

Painting & Finishing

Professional interior painting with computer-matched colors and textures. Priming, sealing, and multi-coat application for a flawless finish.

Trim & Cabinetry

Baseboards, crown molding, door casings, and custom cabinetry. We match existing profiles and finishes so new work blends with the original construction.

Insulation & Vapor Barriers

Mold-resistant insulation and vapor barrier systems that protect against future moisture intrusion. Properly insulated and sealed for energy efficiency.

Plumbing & HVAC

Repair or replacement of plumbing and HVAC components affected by mold or water damage. We fix the source to ensure the problem doesn't come back.
