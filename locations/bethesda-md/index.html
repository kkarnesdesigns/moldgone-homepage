Why Mold Is Common in Bethesda Properties

Bethesda's housing stock includes a mix of historic properties, mid-century colonials, and newer construction. Many of these properties have basements, crawlspaces, and aging plumbing that create the moisture conditions mold needs to thrive.

Montgomery County's humid summers and seasonal storms compound the problem. Water intrusion from heavy rains, poor drainage around foundations, and condensation in poorly ventilated spaces all contribute to mold growth that can go undetected for months.

MoldGone has been serving Bethesda and surrounding Montgomery County communities for over 20 years. We understand the local building styles, common moisture issues, and what it takes to keep properties in this area mold-free.
