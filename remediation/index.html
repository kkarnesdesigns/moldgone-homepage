Our Remediation Process

From Assessment to Clearance

Our proven remediation process follows IICRC S520 standards at every step, ensuring thorough decontamination and verified results.

01

Assessment & Scope

Detailed inspection to identify mold species, extent of contamination, moisture sources, and develop a remediation plan.

02

Containment Setup

Polyethylene barriers, negative air machines, and HEPA scrubbers isolate the work area to prevent spore migration.

03

Removal & Treatment

Contaminated materials are safely removed. All surfaces are HEPA vacuumed, treated with antimicrobials, and decontaminated.

04

Clearance Testing

Independent third-party air quality testing confirms mold levels are at or below normal. We don't stop until you pass.

Types of Mold We Handle

Expert Removal for Every Mold Situation

From common household mold to toxic species, our certified team has the training and equipment to handle any mold challenge safely and effectively.

Black Mold (Stachybotrys)

The most concerning species, often found after prolonged water damage. Requires specialized containment and removal protocols due to toxic mycotoxins.

Aspergillus & Penicillium

Common indoor molds that trigger allergies and respiratory issues. Found on walls, insulation, HVAC systems, and water-damaged materials throughout the home.

Cladosporium

Commonly found on fabrics, wood surfaces, and HVAC ducts. While less dangerous, large colonies still require professional remediation for complete removal.

Crawlspace & Basement Mold

High-humidity environments beneath your home are breeding grounds. We address the mold, seal moisture entry points, and recommend vapor barriers.

Attic & Roof Mold

Poor ventilation and roof leaks cause attic mold infestations. We remediate the mold and address the underlying ventilation or moisture issues.

Commercial Properties

Office buildings, retail spaces, and multifamily units require discreet, efficient remediation. We work around your schedule to minimize business disruption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mold Remediation FAQs

Answers to the most common questions we hear from homeowners and property managers about the remediation process.

Most residential projects take 3-5 business days from containment setup through clearance testing. The exact timeline depends on the size of the affected area, the type of materials involved, and the severity of contamination. We provide a detailed project timeline during the initial assessment so you know exactly what to expect.
In most cases, you can stay in your home as long as the affected area is properly contained and isolated. Our containment barriers and negative air pressure systems prevent mold spores from migrating to other areas. However, for extensive contamination or if family members have severe mold sensitivities, we may recommend temporary relocation for the duration of the project.
Many homeowner's insurance policies cover mold remediation when it results from a covered peril, such as a burst pipe or storm damage. Policies vary widely, so we recommend contacting your insurer early. MoldGone works directly with insurance companies, handling documentation, photo evidence, and claims paperwork to maximize your coverage and minimize out-of-pocket costs.
"Mold removal" implies eliminating all mold, which is impossible -- mold spores exist naturally in every environment. "Mold remediation" is the industry-standard term for returning mold levels to normal, safe concentrations. This involves removing contaminated materials, treating affected surfaces, improving air quality, and addressing the moisture source that caused the growth in the first place.
We arrange independent third-party post-remediation verification (PRV) testing. A separate certified inspector collects air and surface samples after our work is complete. These samples are analyzed by an accredited laboratory. The project isn't considered complete until lab results confirm mold spore levels are at or below normal outdoor baseline levels.
The EPA recommends professional remediation for any mold area larger than 10 square feet. DIY cleanup without proper containment can spread spores throughout your home, making the problem significantly worse. Without industrial HEPA equipment and proper protocols, you risk cross-contaminating clean areas and exposing yourself to concentrated mold spores. Professional remediation ensures the job is done safely and completely.
Cost varies based on the size of the affected area, the type and severity of mold, accessibility, and whether reconstruction is needed afterward. We provide free, detailed estimates after an on-site assessment -- no hidden fees, no surprises. Our pricing is transparent and competitive, and we work with your insurance carrier to minimize your out-of-pocket expense.
