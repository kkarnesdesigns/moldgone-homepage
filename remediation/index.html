What Is Mold Remediation?

More Than Cleaning. It's Restoring Healthy Air.

Mold thrives after water damage, persistent leaks, flooding, and high humidity. Remediation means returning mold to safe, normal levels, what professionals call "normal fungal ecology." It's extreme cleaning at the microscopic level: identifying why mold is growing, stopping the spread, and making the environment safe to breathe in again.

After an in-person consultation, our team drafts a customized remediation protocol. Every worksite is completely contained using state-of-the-art equipment. Following treatment, all surfaces are cleaned with a broad-spectrum disinfectant, leaving your property sparkling clean.

  • Root cause identification: we find why mold is growing, not just where
  • Full containment with negative air pressure to prevent cross-contamination
  • HEPA air filtration running continuously to capture airborne spores
  • Broad-spectrum antimicrobial treatment and deodorization
  • Reconstruction available for any demolition required during the process

The Mold Removal Process

Five Steps to a Mold-Free Property

After an in-person consultation, our team drafts a customized remediation protocol outlining treatment methods, materials, and pricing before work begins.

01

Identify the Source

We trace the moisture source, whether it's a pipe leak, roof damage, flooding, or condensation, and map all impacted areas to determine the full scope of work.

02

Contain the Area

The affected area is sealed off with polyethylene barriers to prevent spores from contaminating other parts of your home or business.

03

Remove & Treat

Contaminated materials are safely removed. All remaining surfaces are treated with antimicrobials as outlined in the remediation protocol.

04

Clean the Air

HEPA air filtration devices capture airborne spores and particles, ensuring contaminants are not transferred through the air during or after removal.

05

Sanitize & Restore

Every surface inside and around the contained area is cleaned with a broad-spectrum disinfectant. Deodorization eliminates lingering odors with fogging equipment.
